# MacMusic manual: figure plan

Where the owner's screenshots go in the manual, how each is cropped,
and which pictures are still needed. They are kept outside the
repository:
- **S01-S44:** the first set, in `~/MacPlus/Screenshots/`, numbered in
  time order (11:41:47 to 11:48:46, 2026-10-08);
- **D01-D28:** a second set (12:14 to 13:04), named by what they show
  (the table at the end of the next section); moved from the desktop to
  `~/MacPlus/Screenshots/` on 2026-10-08.

## How the shots are used

The shots were taken with Mini vMac at an exact 4x zoom, so each one
holds the Mac Plus screen pixel for pixel: the 512 x 342 one-bit screen
is recovered exactly, with nothing of the host window around it. Every
figure is made from that exact screen, in one of five crops:

| Crop | Size (Mac pixels) | Used for | Like the references' |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F, full screen** | 512 x 342 | where the context matters: the Finder, a menu over the window, a dialog box in front of the MacMusic window | MacProject's labelled window (MJ 14), MacWrite's screen pictures (MW 11-16) |
| **W, the MacMusic window** | about 205 x 322 | MacMusic's screen together with its click wheel: learning the controls | the window crops (MW 16) |
| **L, the LCD alone** | about 170 x 140 | one of MacMusic's screens, printed larger; a series of small ones side by side on task pages | the small crops at a task column's foot (MW 56, MD 32) |
| **M, a menu** | the menu and its title | a pulled-down menu, for the reference chapter | MacWrite's menu pictures (MW 119) |
| **D, a dialog or alert** | the box alone | dialog boxes and alerts in the reference chapter and Appendix A | MacProject's dialogs (MJ 118) |

As the style guide says (§8.1), figures are printed at a modest size, and
a partial screen may end in a torn edge (MW 16). Callouts use margin
labels with leader lines (§8.2).

## Where each shot goes

Chapters follow the style guide's structure (§5): an orientation and a
tutorial (Chapter 1), task pages (Chapter 2), the reference (Chapter 3),
then the appendices and glossary. The outline (Phase 3) will fix the
section names.

## Problems with the shots

1. **The old library name.** S01, S29, S33, S43 and S35 show the
   library's name from before Fix 2, "iPod Library". S01 is replaced by
   D01. The owner decided (2026-10-08) to keep the other four as they
   are for now, since this manual is for the owner's own use.
2. **A song title.** S23, S38, S40 and D16 list the song "Wet Dream" (a real
   title by Wet Leg). It's harmless, but a reader may raise an eyebrow.
   Retaking with a different song showing would avoid it. It's your
   call.
3. **One song's title changed between the sets.** The first set shows
   "Good Luck Babe"; the newer shots (D16, D17, D20) show "Good Luck,
   Babe" (edited, nearer the record's own title). Within a chapter the
   pictures should agree, so the tour (Chapter 1), which uses the first
   set, keeps "Good Luck Babe" throughout, and the text quotes whichever
   a figure shows. A retake of the tour's few shots would make the whole
   manual agree.
4. **Personal details in D27.** The TeachText letter in D27 shows what
   looks like a real postal address. For a manual kept privately it's
   harmless; for anything shared, it should go. A retake with neutral
   text (an everyday note, in the spirit of MacProject's kitchen-counter
   note, MJ 3) is better than blanking the lines, which would look
   edited (F7).
5. **The pointer** shows in most shots, sometimes over MacMusic's screen (S11,
   S12, S13). Of these, the references show it in tutorials (MJ 14) but
   rarely in small crops. Small crops will be made without it where it
   sits over the LCD. As it's a pixel picture, the pointer can be
   painted out exactly.
