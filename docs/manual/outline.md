sections, in the shape the style guide sets (§5): learning, using,
reference, then appendices, glossary and index. Each section says what
it covers, its figures (codes from `docs/manual/figures.md`), and where
its facts come from (`docs/user-guide.md`, `docs/decisions.md`, the
code). Page counts are estimates, for a 7½ x 9 in page.

page, then an orientation and numbered lessons in the MacProject manner

in everyday terms, before any steps:
- your record shelf: the **MacMusic Library**, a folder on your disk;
- a mix tape: a **playlist**, which lists songs without moving them;

## Appendixes (about 12 pages)

- **Appendix A, Messages:** every alert and message MacMusic can show,
  quoted exactly, grouped as below, each with what it means and what to
  do. The nine pictured ones are shown small beside their entries
  (S31, S34, D07, D19, D20, D21, D22, D23, D24). The groups:
  - starting up and libraries (about 12 messages: not found, not
    inside a library, could not be opened or created, "the library
    location could not be remembered", not enough memory to read the
    library, skipped files);
  - importing and exporting (about 15: not a WAV file, the
    wrong-kind-of-WAV message as text (F1), already in the library,
    full at 500 songs, not enough space, file couldn't be moved,
    exported but ...);
  - playlists (about 9: names, the 10-playlist limit, already there,
    could not be created, renamed, deleted, or changed);
  - songs and playback (about 7: details couldn't be saved, rating
    couldn't be saved, a song's file couldn't be read, sound playback
    isn't available).
- **Appendix B, About Sound Files:** mono only, as the Macintosh has one sound channel (MU6); the files MacMusic plays (WAV,
  22,050 samples a second, 8-bit, mono) and why it's the Macintosh
  Plus's own sound; how much room music takes (about 1.3 MB a minute,
  with examples: about 30 seconds on an 800K floppy, about 15 minutes
  on a 20 MB hard disk, about 25 hours on the largest volume System 6
  can use, 2 GB); what MacMusic says when a file won't do. Preparing files and
  getting them onto the Mac: see *MacMusic Utilities* (MU1, MU2).
- **Appendix C, Keyboard and Click Wheel Summary:** one page, with
  keycap drawings: every control by mouse and by keyboard.
- **Appendix D, Troubleshooting:** no sound (including something plugged into the sound output port, MU6); the library can't be
  found (the hard disk not switched on or connected, or a floppy not
  inserted); a file MacMusic won't import (see *MacMusic Utilities*); the music pauses while another program
  works hard; not enough memory under MultiFinder; scanning a large
  library takes a while; songs play a little fast; the quietest volume
  isn't silent; "a song's file couldn't be read".

Sources: `src/*.c` (every `Message_Show` and alert, about 45),
`resources/MacMusic.r` (the alerts), the issues log's "Known issues in
1.0", the user guide's "Limits and known issues".

## Size

About 94 pages: front matter 6, Chapter 1 22, Chapter 2 26, Chapter 3
20, appendixes 12, back matter 8. That's above MM7's estimate (60-80),
mainly because the owner's screenshots support full task pages. It's in
line with the references (MacProject 142 and MacDraw 123 pages).

## Decisions

**Decided 2026-10-08:** the outline and O1-O6 approved as recommended.


- **O1, the period habits:** keep lessons 1 and 2, making a backup copy
  of the MacMusic disk and installing on a hard disk, as every reference
  begins. The release's 800K floppy makes them real. *Recommended.*
- **O2, the reader comment page:** keep it, as the references' manuals
  carry one, but address it to the project rather than to a company,
  with no postal address. Or leave it out. *Recommended: keep, as a
  period touch.*
- **O3, "If You've Used a Pocket Music Player":** a short section in About This
  Manual, modelled on MacDraw's word to MacPaint users. *Recommended.*
- **O4, Appendix B (reversed 2026-10-08, MU1):** "About Sound Files",
  on the Plus only; converting songs is left to *MacMusic Utilities*.
- **O5, the size:** about 94 pages, as above, or trimmed towards MM7's
  60-80 by merging task pages. *Recommended: about 94.*
- **O6, the order of work (Phase 6):** Chapter 1 first (it sets the
  voice), then Chapter 2, Chapter 3, the appendixes, and the front
  and back matter last, each reviewed by you. *Recommended.*
