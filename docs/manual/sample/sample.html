7Playing a Song

You can move around MacMusic’s menus now, so it’s time to listen to something. Every song in your library is listed by title under Songs, in the Music menu.

Choose Music from the main menu, and then choose Songs.

Turn the click wheel until the item is highlighted, then click the centre button.

The Songs screen lists your songs in alphabetical order.

Highlight a song and click the centre button.

In a few seconds you’ll hear the song through the Macintosh’s speaker, and the Now Playing screen shows what’s playing:

When the song ends, the next one in the list follows it, with no pause between them.

You can also press the Space bar to pause and to play.

Click the bottom of the click wheel to pause.

The play icon changes to two bars. Click again to carry on.

Moving Through a Song

While a song plays, the Now Playing screen lets you skip ahead, go back, or jump to any place in it, and set how loud it is.

  • fast-forward and rewind
  • the scrubber
  • the volume

The music keeps playing while you do any of these.

To Fast-Forward or Rewind

Position the pointer on the right side of the click wheel and hold down the mouse button. To rewind, use the left side.

After half a second the play icon changes to two arrows, and the song moves on at four times its normal speed; after two seconds, sixteen times.

Release the mouse button.

The song carries on from the new place.

OR

Hold down Option and the Right Arrow or Left Arrow key.

To Use the Scrubber

On Now Playing, click the centre button.

A diamond, the scrubber, appears on the progress bar.

Turn the click wheel.

One full turn moves through the whole song. When you stop turning, the song jumps to the new place.

The scrubber goes away by itself after five seconds.

To Change the Volume

On Now Playing, turn the click wheel: clockwise for louder, counterclockwise for softer.

The volume bar takes the place of the progress bar. One full turn goes from the softest setting to the loudest.

OR

Press the Down Arrow key for louder, the Up Arrow key for softer, one step at a time.

MacMusic remembers the volume for next time.
