■ MacMusic Quick Reference Card

The MacMusic utilities on your Mac, and MacMusic on the Macintosh Plus

A song’s journey

  1. Convert it to a MacMusic WAV with the MacMusic Converter Utility.
  2. Put it on a disk for the Plus with the MacMusic Transfer Utility: HD60_512 Transfer.hda.
  3. Shut the Plus down (Shut Down, in the Special menu), switch it off, and put its SD card in your Mac.
  4. Back the card up with the Macintosh Backup Utility: Back Up Now, then Back Up.
  5. Copy the disk image onto the card’s top level; eject the card, put it back in the BlueSCSI, and switch the Plus on.
  6. Import the song in MacMusic from the Transfer disk’s WAV Files folder (page 4).

MacMusic Converter Utility

To convert a sound fileClick Local File, then Choose File; or drag the song onto the window. Click MacMusic WAV, then Convert.
To convert a YouTube videoClick YouTube, paste the address, click Look Up (or press Return), then Convert. Only videos you own or may save.
To make an MP3 tooUnder Output, click Both (or MP3 for the MP3 alone). An MP3 made from an MP3 asks first: Re-encode, WAV Only or Cancel.
To set the title MacMusic showsType it in Title. File name, left empty, follows the title; Saves as shows the names.
To even out the loudnessTurn on Normalise. Its level is under Advanced, Normalise to (−1.0 dBFS to start).
To trim silence at the endsTurn on Trim silence at the ends, and choose 2 or 3 seconds in Longer than.
To choose where files goUnder Save To, click Choose. Use Default: the MacMusic Converter folder in your Music folder.
To find what was madeClick Show in Finder beside the file in Results.
To cancelClick Cancel, or press Esc. Nothing half-made is kept.
If a file of that name is thereKeep Both (the next free name), Replace, or Cancel.

A MacMusic WAV is mono, 22,050 samples a second, 8-bit: about 1.3 MB a minute. Its name is at most 31 characters, with .wav.

On your Mac

The MacMusic Transfer Utility and the Macintosh Backup Utility

MacMusic Transfer Utility

To add songsDrag MacMusic WAV files, or a folder of them, onto the window; or click Add Files. Remove and Clear All take them off the list.
To choose the SCSI IDChoose 0 to 6 from the SCSI ID menu (6 to start). Each disk image on the card needs an ID of its own; 0 is usually your startup disk.
To make the disk imageClick Build Transfer Image. It’s saved in the MacMusic Transfer folder in your Music folder.
To copy it to the cardClick Show in Finder, and drag the image onto the card’s top level, not into a folder.
To see what was checkedClick What was checked, under Result.
To cancelClick Cancel, or press Esc. Nothing is saved.
If a song can’t be usedIt isn’t a MacMusic WAV: make one with the Converter, and add that.
If a name is changedShown in orange under On the Mac: 31 characters at most, no colons, Clash.wav beside clash.wav becomes Clash 2.wav.

Macintosh Backup Utility

Before the card comes outShut the Plus down with Shut Down, in the Special menu, and switch it off when it says you may.
To back up the cardPut it in your Mac. Click Back Up Now, then Back Up. Nothing is ever written to the card.
To register a new cardThe first time it’s inserted: type a name, and click Register.
To put the card backClick Eject; put it in the BlueSCSI before you switch the Plus on.
To restore a disk imageSelect a snapshot, click Restore…, then Chosen files and folders; tick the image, choose a folder, click Restore. Then drag it onto the card.
To restore everythingRestore…, The whole snapshot, into a new folder.
If the card itself has failedFormat a new card in Disk Utility (ExFAT, Master Boot Record); Control-click a snapshot, Restore to a New Card….
To check a snapshotSelect it, and click Verify Backup.
To keep or delete a snapshotControl-click it: Keep (Never Thin), or Delete… (to the Trash).
To change the settingsSettings, in the Macintosh Backup Utility menu (⌘,): the backup folder, and thinning.

Backups are kept in Mac Plus Backups, in your home folder. Thinning keeps a card’s newest snapshot of each day for a week, each week for a month, each month for a year; at least two, and any marked Keep.

MacMusic on the Macintosh Plus

Adding songs, the menus, and playlists

Adding songs from the Transfer disk

To import a songChoose Import Song from the File menu; open the Transfer disk, then WAV Files; select the song, and click Open.
“Importing from another disk will take some time”Click Import.
“The file will be moved”Click OK to move it into your library, or Just Copy to leave it on the Transfer disk too.
The song’s detailsType or change them (63 characters each), and click Import.
“Import another song?”Click Import Another for the next song, or Done when you’ve finished.

The menus

FileImport Song…, Export Song… (gives the song back as a WAV file, and takes it out of the library), Choose Library…, Rescan Library, Library Info…, Page Setup…, Print Library… (⌘P), the libraries used before, Quit (⌘Q)
EditEdit Song…: the highlighted song’s details, or Now Playing’s
PlaylistsNew Playlist…, Rename…, Delete…, Remove from Playlist, Add to “name”
AppleAbout MacMusic…, and your desk accessories: Control Panel sets the speaker volume, Alarm Clock the clock

Playlists

To make oneChoose New Playlist… from the Playlists menu, and type a name.
To add a songHighlight it, and choose Add to “name” from the Playlists menu.
To take a song offIn the playlist, highlight it, and choose Remove from Playlist. It stays in the library.

Printing (MacMusic 1.1)

To print a list of the libraryChoose Print Library… (⌘P); tick Songs, Albums or Artists (with each album’s songs, or each artist’s albums), click Print, then OK.
To choose the paperChoose Page Setup…: A4 or US Letter. The first print asks for it.
To stop printing⌘ .. The music pauses while it prints.

Limits: 500 songs and 10 playlists in a library; 640K under MultiFinder (it works in 512K); MacMusic WAV files only, mono, 22,050 Hz, 8-bit.
