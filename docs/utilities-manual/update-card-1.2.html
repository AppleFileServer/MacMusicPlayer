MacMusic 1.2 Update

What’s new since MacMusic 1.1

MacMusic 1.2 plays songs at the Macintosh Plus’s own rate, lists an artist’s or a genre’s songs album by album, and makes the song details window easier to click. Everything else works as before.

Songs at their true speed

22,254 Hz songsMacMusic now plays WAV files made at 22,254 samples a second, the Plus’s own rate, as well as 22,050. They play at their true speed and pitch. Guide, page 68.
The MacMusic Converter 1.1Makes its MacMusic WAVs at 22,254, and brings every song to the same loudness (MacMusic Utilities, Chapter 2).
Songs at 22,050Play as before, about 0.9% fast; the times shown are right. To have one at its true speed, convert it again and import it. Guide, page 72.
ImportingImport Song takes either rate. A WAV of any other kind is refused, with a message saying what’s needed. Guide, page 34.

All, album by album

All in an Albums listGives the songs album by album, each album in track order, as you’d play the records. That’s All in Albums, and All in an artist’s or a genre’s albums. Guide, page 25.
SongsMusic > Songs still lists every song by title.

Smaller changes

The song details windowA click anywhere inside a box’s frame now selects that box, even right at its edge. Before, a click near the frame missed, and the typing stayed in the last box. Guide, page 36.
Edit Song and ratingsKeep each song’s rate when they save its details.

Moving up from MacMusic 1.1

Your songs, playlists and settings come with you

Installing MacMusic 1.2

To install itQuit MacMusic. Drag the new MacMusic from its disk to where the old one is on your hard disk, and click OK when the Finder asks to replace it.
Your librariesStay as they are: songs, playlists, ratings and the libraries MacMusic remembers. So do your settings.
The first startAs quick as before: MacMusic 1.2 uses 1.1’s note of your songs. Guide, page 47.
MemoryAs 1.1: 640K under MultiFinder, at least 512K.

Going back to 1.1 or 1.0

Songs at 22,254 HzMacMusic 1.0 and 1.1 can’t play them: they leave them out, saying some files weren’t recognised. The files aren’t changed, and the songs are back when 1.2 opens the library again. Guide, page 73.
The first start in 1.1Reads every song file once, as 1.1 doesn’t know 1.2’s note of your songs.
Songs at 22,050 HzAre the same in every version, so they can go back and forth.

What hasn’t changed

The iPodIts screens, the click wheel, Now Playing, Shuffle and Repeat, the Clock.
The library500 songs and 10 playlists at most; printing, as in 1.1.
The utilitiesThe Converter 1.1 makes songs for 1.2. The Transfer Utility 1.0 takes only 22,050 Hz songs for now.
