MacMusic 1.1 Update

What’s new since MacMusic 1.0

MacMusic 1.1 starts more quickly, prints your library, and imports several songs one after another. Everything else works as before.

Starting faster

A note of your songsMacMusic keeps a note of each song in your library, so it needn’t open every song file each time it starts. On a Macintosh Plus a library of 300 songs starts in about 13 seconds. Guide, page 47.
“Loading the library…”Shown under the Apple logo while MacMusic reads your library, each time it starts and after you import songs. Guide, page 72.
The first start, and Rescan LibraryRead every song file, and take longer. MacMusic reads a song again by itself whenever its file has changed. Guide, page 40.

Printing your library

Print Library… (⌘P)Prints a list of your songs, your albums (with their songs, if you like) or your artists (with their albums), with the library’s name and the date at the top of each page. Guide, page 41.
Page Setup…Chooses the paper: A4 to begin with, or US Letter. The first print asks for it. Guide, page 41.
PrintersWhichever the Chooser has: an ImageWriter or a LaserWriter, on the printer port or AppleTalk. The music pauses while it prints; ⌘ . stops. Guide, page 41.

Importing several songs

“Import another song?”Asked after each song you import. Click Import Another to choose the next, or Done when you’ve finished. Guide, page 34.
Each songIs in your library as soon as it’s imported; the library is read once, at the end. Cancel in a song’s details skips just that song.

Smaller changes

Under MultiFinderMacMusic’s window comes to the front as soon as it starts, instead of staying behind the Finder’s while the library loads.
Create New LibraryThe Save dialog box’s prompt, “New library in:”, is shown whole.

Moving up from MacMusic 1.0

Your songs, playlists and settings come with you

Installing MacMusic 1.1

To install itQuit MacMusic. Drag the new MacMusic from its disk to where the old one is on your hard disk, and click OK when the Finder asks to replace it.
Your librariesStay as they are: songs, playlists, ratings and the libraries MacMusic remembers. So do your settings.
The first startMacMusic reads every song in the library once, to make its note: an invisible folder called MacMusic Cache, inside the library. After that it starts more quickly. Guide, page 47.
MemoryAs 1.0: 640K under MultiFinder, at least 512K.

Going back to 1.0

A library used by 1.1Opens in MacMusic 1.0 as before; 1.0 doesn’t notice the MacMusic Cache folder.
Your song filesAre the same in both versions, so songs can go back and forth between them.

What hasn’t changed

The iPodIts screens, the click wheel, Now Playing, Shuffle and Repeat, the Clock.
The library500 songs and 10 playlists at most; MacMusic WAV files, mono, 22,050 Hz, 8-bit.
The utilitiesThe MacMusic Converter, Transfer and Backup Utilities work with 1.1 as with 1.0.
